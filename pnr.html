Layout?
Cells abut into rows. Every cell is 72 lambda tall with vdd and vss at the same height, so rows tile exactly.
Abstract is the boundary and the pins; Detail is every mask layer.
Route adds the wires to this drawing; Routing hides and shows them.

Press Place to lay the netlist out.

Fabrication?
The mask steps that build the silicon, in process order: wafer, well, diffusions, poly, contacts, metal.
The ideal pair is one NMOS and one PMOS - the process with no design in it.
A cut takes the stack under one placed cell, wires included once you have pressed Route.
